About InternGuide

One reliable workflow for every internship journey

InternGuide connects students, recruiters, and universities in a secure platform built around verified opportunities, clear applications, and accountable decisions.

Our Purpose

Making internship opportunities easier to trust and manage

InternGuide replaces disconnected messages and paperwork with a single, transparent process from opportunity posting through university approval and student application.

Student-first discovery

Students can find approved internships, apply with their resume, and keep their university profile current.

Recruiter efficiency

Recruiters can post opportunities, manage listings, and review applicants from a focused dashboard.

University confidence

Universities verify students and approve internship opportunities before they appear publicly.

Built For Accountability

A practical platform with the right safeguards

Secure access

Role-based JWT authentication keeps each workspace focused and protected.

Resume applications

Students can submit applications with the documents recruiters need.

Approval workflow

Only approved opportunities are presented in public internship browsing.

Audit history

Important approval actions are recorded for clarity and review.
